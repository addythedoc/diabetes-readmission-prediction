# Visualization Outputs

This folder contains key exploratory and model-related visualizations generated during the analysis of the Diabetes Readmission Prediction project.

### 1. `readmission_proportion_by_age.png`
- Shows the proportion of 30-day readmissions across age groups.
- Helps identify age patterns in hospital readmission risk.

### 2. `rf_variable_importance_top20.png`
- Top 20 predictors ranked by Random Forest Mean Decrease Gini.
- Highlights the strongest contributors to readmission prediction.

### 3. `time_in_hospital_by_readmission.png`
- Compares hospital stay length between patients who were readmitted vs. not readmitted.
- Useful for visualizing distributional differences between outcome groups.

---

These figures are automatically generated by the script:
`/scripts/02_eda.R` and `/scripts/03_model.R`.
